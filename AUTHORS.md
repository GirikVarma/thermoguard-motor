# Project authorship and attribution

**Project owner and submitter: Girik Varma (GirikVarma).**

ThermoGuard Motor's application, training pipeline, experiment controls, and documentation were created for this project with AI assistance from OpenAI Codex.

The project uses established neural-network and thermal-history techniques. It makes no claim to invent these techniques, to be the first motor-temperature estimator, or to have collected the source motor measurements.

The measured motor dataset is by Wilhelm Kirchgassner, Oliver Wallscheid, and Joachim Bocker at Paderborn University. Its attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 terms remain in **data/ATTRIBUTION.md**. The transformed replay samples retain that license. Related research appears in **docs/references.md**.

This repository has no blanket license for newly authored software. The dataset license should not be interpreted as a general license for every file.
